💡 How to change permissions:

🌐 In Desktop Browser (Chrome / Edge / Brave):
  1. Look at the left side of the address bar next to localhost:5000.
  2. Click the Padlock (🔒) or Tune (🎛️) icon.
  3. Switch Camera, Microphone, and Notifications to Allow.
  4. Refresh page to apply.
📱 On Android Mobile Device:
  1. Open phone Settings → Apps → ChitChat.
  2. Tap Permissions.
  3. Allow Camera, Microphone, and Notifications.
